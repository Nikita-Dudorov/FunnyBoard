A few good keys.

Just start typing. Your wooden mechanical sounds are ready to go.

  1. Find your sound. Open the profile menu to try six different moods.
  2. Make a key your own. Click a key below your note, then choose a sound or upload an audio file. Use “Pick any key” for any key, including Esc; click the picking button again to cancel. Click the selected key again or use “Clear selection” to deselect it. The app starts with no key selected.
  3. Take it everywhere. In the desktop app, switch to Everywhere to hear sounds in other apps, even when FunnyBoard is minimized. Close the window to keep it in your tray. Use the tray menu to mute or quit.
  4. Keep what you make. Notes and bindings save on this device. Download your note as a text file, or export a profile with its audio to back it up.

Ctrl / ⌘ + Shift + M toggles sound

Everywhere uses physical key positions. It never records what you type in other apps. macOS requires Accessibility access; Linux requires an X11 session.
